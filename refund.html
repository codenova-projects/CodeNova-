Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last Updated: January 2024

1. Overview

CodeNova Solutions is committed to customer satisfaction. This Refund & Cancellation Policy outlines our terms regarding project cancellation and refund eligibility. We strive to deliver exceptional service, and in the rare event you're not satisfied, we have a fair refund process.

2. Refund Eligibility

Refunds may be issued in the following circumstances:

  • Client-initiated cancellation before work commencement
  • Significant project delays beyond agreed timelines (more than 30 days)
  • Failure to deliver agreed deliverables due to CodeNova's fault
  • Non-compliance with contractual obligations by CodeNova
  • Payment errors or duplicate charges

3. Refund Ineligibility

Refunds will NOT be provided in the following cases:

  • Cancellation after work has commenced (partial payment applies)
  • Cancellation due to client's lack of decision-making or project direction changes
  • Dissatisfaction with completed work delivered as per specifications
  • Changes in client requirements after project start
  • Client's failure to provide necessary information or resources
  • Third-party integration failures beyond CodeNova's control
  • Cancellation after 50% project completion

4. Cancellation Process

4.1 How to Request Cancellation

To cancel a project, the client must submit a written cancellation request to CodeNova Solutions via email at codenovasolutions@gmail.com with the following information:

  • Project ID or name
  • Reason for cancellation
  • Current project status and percentage completion

4.2 Cancellation Processing Time

Cancellation requests will be processed within 5 business days. CodeNova will confirm the cancellation status and issue refunds (if applicable) within 14 business days of approval.

5. Refund Amount Calculation

The refund amount depends on the project's progress at the time of cancellation:

  • Before Work Starts (0%): 100% refund of advance payment
  • Work Commenced (0-25% complete): 75% refund (25% retention for initial work)
  • Moderate Progress (25-50% complete): 50% refund
  • Advanced Progress (50%+ complete): No refund; client pays for work completed

Note: All refunds are calculated after deducting any third-party costs incurred (hosting, domain registration, API services, etc.).

6. Partial Refund Scenarios

In cases where a partial refund is applicable, the following will be charged:

  • Actual development time at the project rate
  • Any third-party service subscriptions or integrations
  • Infrastructure and hosting costs incurred
  • License or premium tool costs

A detailed breakdown of charges will be provided with the refund decision.

7. Refund Methods

Refunds will be issued through the same payment method used for the original transaction:

  • Bank transfer refunds: 7-10 business days
  • Card refunds: 10-15 business days (depends on card issuer)
  • Payment gateway refunds: 5-7 business days

Please note that banking institutions may take additional time to process the refund to your account.

8. Project Delays and Compensation

While we strive to meet all deadlines, if CodeNova delays project delivery beyond 30 days of the agreed timeline:

  • Client may request a project status report
  • CodeNova will provide a revised completion date
  • If delay exceeds 60 days, client may cancel and receive a partial or full refund (at CodeNova's discretion)
  • Delays caused by client factors do not qualify for refund eligibility

9. Quality Guarantees

CodeNova guarantees:

  • All deliverables will be tested and debugged before submission
  • Code will follow industry best practices and standards
  • Documentation will be clear and comprehensive
  • Support will be provided for 30 days post-delivery for bug fixes

If deliverables fail to meet agreed specifications, CodeNova will make corrections at no additional cost. If corrections are not possible within 14 days, a partial or full refund may be considered.

10. Scope Changes and Additional Charges

If the client requests changes or additions to the original project scope:

  • Scope changes must be documented in writing
  • Additional charges will be quoted separately
  • Original refund terms apply only to the original scope
  • Additional work is subject to separate payment terms

11. Intellectual Property Rights Upon Cancellation

Upon project cancellation and refund:

  • Client retains rights to any completed deliverables for which payment was received
  • Incomplete code or partial work reverts to CodeNova
  • Third-party assets or licenses are transferred only if fully licensed to client
  • CodeNova retains rights to general methodologies and processes developed

12. Disputes and Appeals

If you dispute a refund decision:

  • Submit an appeal within 7 days of the refund decision
  • Provide detailed reasons and supporting documentation
  • CodeNova will review and respond within 7 days
  • If still unresolved, disputes may be escalated to mediation

13. Maintenance and Support Cancellation

For ongoing maintenance and support contracts:

  • Cancellation notice: 30 days required
  • No refund for support already provided
  • Unused support hours may be credited toward future projects
  • Early termination may incur a 15% early termination fee

14. Government & Compliance

This refund policy complies with applicable laws including:

  • Indian Consumer Protection Act, 2019
  • Information Technology Act, 2000
  • E-commerce Guidelines and regulations
  • RBI guidelines on payment processing

15. Policy Changes

CodeNova reserves the right to modify this refund policy. Any changes will be communicated to clients at least 14 days in advance. Ongoing projects will follow the policy in effect at the time of project initiation.

17. Satisfaction Guarantee

We're confident in the quality of our work. If you're not satisfied with the final deliverables and your concerns are valid according to this policy, we'll work with you to find a resolution. Your satisfaction is our priority.
