Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers to common questions about our services

What are your pricing models? +

We offer transparent, fixed pricing for standard services. Website Development starts at ₹7,999 (Basic) to ₹14,999 (Premium). Web Applications range from ₹24,999 (Starter) to ₹49,999 (Professional). Custom software development starts at ₹39,999. View our complete pricing page for all services and packages.

How do I place an order and what happens next? +

Contact us via email (sales.codenova@gmail.com), phone (+91 9677819018), or WhatsApp (+91 75399 00984). We'll discuss your requirements and provide a detailed quote. After you approve, we'll send an invoice and setup a secure payment link. Once payment is received, development begins immediately.

What payment methods do you accept? +

We accept multiple payment methods: Bank Transfer (NEFT/RTGS), UPI, Airpay Secure Payment, and Invoice-based payments. All transactions are secure and processed through verified payment gateways. GST invoices are provided for all payments.

What is your typical project timeline? +

Timelines vary by project: Basic websites (5-7 days), Premium websites (12-15 days), Starter web apps (20-30 days), Professional web apps (45-60 days). Custom software development timelines are discussed during project planning. We ensure on-time delivery through our proven development process.

Who owns the code and intellectual property? +

Upon full payment, you own all developed code, designs, and deliverables. We provide source code, documentation, and all necessary files. You have the right to use, modify, and deploy the software. Ownership terms are clearly stated in our project agreement.

Do you provide after-delivery support? +

Yes! All projects include 30 days of free support for bug fixes and minor adjustments. Ongoing maintenance, technical support, and enhancements are available through our AMC (Annual Maintenance Contract) packages starting at ₹799/hour.

What is your refund policy? +

Refunds depend on project stage: 100% refund if cancelled before work starts, 75% if cancelled within 25% completion, 50% if cancelled before 50% completion. No refund after 50% completion. Exceptions apply for CodeNova's non-compliance. View full refund policy.

Do you handle existing code and debugging? +

Yes! We specialize in debugging, optimization, and refactoring existing projects. We can fix bugs, optimize performance, add features, migrate code, or completely redesign existing applications. Hourly rates and project-based pricing are available.

Is my data and payment secure? +

Absolutely. We use industry-standard SSL/TLS encryption for all data transmission. Payments are processed through secure, verified payment gateways (Airpay, Bank Transfer). Your confidential information is protected by our comprehensive Privacy Policy and NDA agreements.

Do you provide GST invoices? +

Yes, GST-registered invoices are provided for all projects. All amounts quoted include GST where applicable. Invoices are issued upon payment confirmation and include itemized service breakdowns for your records and accounting purposes.
