Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers to common questions about our services

What is your typical project timeline? +

Project timelines vary based on complexity and scope. Small projects typically take 1-2 weeks, medium projects 3-8 weeks, and larger enterprise solutions 2-6 months. We'll provide accurate estimates after understanding your requirements.

Do you offer post-deployment support? +

Absolutely! We provide comprehensive post-deployment support including bug fixes, performance optimization, security updates, and feature enhancements. Support packages are customizable based on your needs.

Can you work on existing projects? +

Yes, we specialize in working with existing codebases. Whether you need debugging, performance optimization, feature additions, or complete refactoring, our team can handle it efficiently.

What is your payment structure? +

Payments are shared securely through official payment links after project confirmation. We offer flexible payment schedules: advance deposit for project start, milestone-based payments during development, and final payment upon completion.

Do you sign NDAs? +

Yes, we take confidentiality seriously and are happy to sign NDAs before discussing your project. Your intellectual property and business information are always protected.

Can you help with programming concepts and tutoring? +

Yes! We offer programming consultation and tutoring services. Whether you're learning a new language or need guidance on best practices, our experts can help you develop your skills.
